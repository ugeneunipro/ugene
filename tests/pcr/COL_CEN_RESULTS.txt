The entire product metadata sets were compared, not just counts. Coordinates,
primer orientation, match lengths and overhangs agree with an independent
position-by-position substitution scan and independent pairing. All products
from each smaller mismatch allowance remain present at the next allowance.
Fixed and uncapped-old product temperatures are identical (Rough calculator).
All original capped products are included in the complete expected set.
Chromosomes 1,2,4,5 and organellar sequences ChrM/ChrC have zero products
with tail=15 and mismatches=0..3. These 24 cases also agree with the oracle.
Whole-genome sequences were processed individually, never concatenated.
All complete results therefore originate from Chr3 under these parameters.

These are binding sites, not PCR products. A product needs an opposing pair
within the allowed length range. Requiring a perfect 3-prime tail filters
individual sites even when their total mismatch count is within allowance.
With one allowed mismatch, the first 50 forward candidates all fail the
15-bp perfect-tail check: this directly explains the old zero-product output.

Retained local artifacts
------------------------
build/debug-pcr/col-cen/ contains the downloaded genome, original data split
by chromosome, runner/oracle sources, compile.sh, analyze.py, binaries, raw
JSON timings and coordinate TSVs. It is an ignored diagnostic directory.
Product exports: Chr3-products-m{0..3}-p{0,15}.tsv.
Binding-site exports: Chr3-binding-sites-m{0..3}-p{0,15}.tsv.
Exported coordinate files with descriptive names use 1-based inclusive
coordinates; raw runner TSVs and oracle TSVs use 0-based starts.

Reproduction from the repository root:
  zsh build/debug-pcr/col-cen/compile.sh src/plugins/pcr/src after
  build/debug-pcr/col-cen/pcr-after build/debug-pcr/col-cen/Chr3.fa 1 15 5000 /tmp/pcr-products.tsv
  python3 build/debug-pcr/col-cen/analyze.py

No GUI or Workflow Designer end-to-end run was performed in this validation.
The historical vector/mutagenesis example cannot be checked without its
input sequences. This report concerns the supplied Col-CEN primers.
